Overview

Concierge sports medicine rooted in pelvis-centered precision

LINK Pro is a Costa Mesa, California practice delivering personalized body treatment for elite athletes and high performers. Care is grounded in osteopathic principles, BioTensegrity, and methodologies developed through mentorship with Guy Voyer, DO.

LINK Pro is a concierge sports medicine and performance studio in Costa Mesa, California. Founded by Dr. Jason Amstutz, DC, the practice treats the body as a dynamic, interconnected system—with the pelvis as a foundational anchor for movement, recovery, and long-term health. Services span comprehensive biomechanical assessment, manual osteopathy, ELDOA and myofascial stretching, post-injury recovery, and professional education for clinicians and coaches. LINK Pro’s patient roster includes NBA and NFL athletes, Olympic hopefuls, and performers who travel specifically for precision-led care.

Dr. Jason Amstutz

Founder & Lead Practitioner

DC, CCSP*, RTP, CSCS

Dr. Jason Amstutz built LINK Pro on a pelvis-centered, whole-body approach to sports medicine. His work integrates musculoskeletal, visceral, and neurological care, informed by years of study under osteopath Guy Voyer, DO. He works with professional athletes across the NBA, NFL, and international sport, and trains clinicians through LINK Pro courses and seminars.

Max Karish

Lead ELDOA Instructor

CMT · Certified ELDOA Trainer · SomaTraining & SomaTherapy Practitioner

Max Karish leads ELDOA and myofascial stretching programming at LINK Pro. A former competitive gymnast and martial artist, he discovered ELDOA while resolving chronic issues conventional care could not explain. He now helps clients decompress the spine, restore posture, and build sustainable movement practice.
