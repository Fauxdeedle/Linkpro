How it all links together

Every method we teach answers the same question from a different angle: how do we restore space, flow, and organization to the whole structure?

01

SomaTherapy

SomaTherapy is the manual therapy arm. The practitioner assesses the body globally, starting at the pelvis, and treats the root cause rather than the site of pain.

02

Osteoarticular Pumping

Osteoarticular Pumping restores fluid dynamics to the joint. Space and flow matter: a joint that moves fluid well is a joint that can heal and adapt.

03

ELDOA

ELDOA carries that work into the patient's own hands. These targeted postures decompress specific joints and tension the fascial chains around them, maintaining the space created in treatment.

04

SomaTraining

SomaTraining builds on that foundation with corrective and performance training that respects the body's structure, so strength is developed across the full kinetic chain instead of loaded on top of dysfunction.

05

Yoga

Yoga adds breath, awareness, and nervous system regulation. It gives clients a sustainable daily practice for self-regulation, reinforcing the fluidity and elasticity the rest of the system works to restore.

Each piece is valuable on its own. Together they form one framework: treat it, decompress it, train it, and integrate it. That's the link.
