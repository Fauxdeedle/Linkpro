## Course outline

### LumboPelvoFemoral focus

We'll dive into anatomy, biomechanics, treatment strategies, and corrective exercises for the LPFemoral complex — but before we meet in person, your team will complete a few online courses to lay the foundation.

### Pelvis / lumbar spine / femur relationship

We'll take a deep dive into the structural and functional relationships between these regions, with a focus on evaluation strategies and corrective work.

These 3 are inseparable: in performance/dynamic, they move together due to innumerable links: think ligaments and fascia!

The pelvis is the key to:

- Lumbar disc pathology
- Hip labrum & impingement
- Adductor tears and sports hernias
- And many other downstream joint dysfunction — knee/ankle/foot

The sacroiliac joints are highly moveable (although on a micro scale, compared with iliofemoral), and therefore are often overlooked, discounted or completely ignored. The layers of information that comes from the SI joint and its capsule, interosseous ligaments, other SIJ ligs and then the layers of the glutes, signify the importance of the pelvis.
