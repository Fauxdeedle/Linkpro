## About Soluna

Soluna is a serene luxury retreat in Pescadero, on the Pacific coast of Baja California Sur, where farmland meets the ocean. With only eight rooms, the retreat will be intimate and personal. A private chef will provide healthy meals and snacks. Between yoga sessions, relax by the pool, book a massage, or visit the beach. Cerritos Beach is about a five-minute drive; Todos Santos is roughly ten to fifteen minutes by car. At least one excursion to Todos Santos is planned.
