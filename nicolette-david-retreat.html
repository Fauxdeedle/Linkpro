Live By The Sun, Love By The Moon

the many faces of hatha yoga

Wednesday, Nov 11 – Sunday, Nov 15 · 4 nights, 5 days
Soluna Estate · El Pescadero, Baja California Sur

The setting

Soluna Estate

Soluna is a serene luxury retreat in Pescadero, on the Pacific coast of Baja California Sur, where farmland meets the ocean. With only eight rooms, the experience stays intimate and personal. A private chef provides healthy meals and snacks. Between yoga sessions, relax by the pool, book a massage, or visit the beach. Cerritos Beach is about a five-minute drive; the historic town of Todos Santos is roughly ten to fifteen minutes by car, with at least one group excursion planned.
